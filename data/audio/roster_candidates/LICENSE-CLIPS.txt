Full-roster candidate clip licensing and attribution
===================================================

The 21 source recordings in originals/ are explicitly CC0 1.0 in the
official FSD50K dev/eval_clips_info_FSD50K.json metadata:
https://zenodo.org/records/4060432

CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

SOURCES.json identifies each contributor, Freesound page, source hash,
exact pinned mirror URL, creator description, and clip license. It includes
three rejected clipped recordings which are never selected for playback.
Originals are untouched FSD50K distributed WAVs, not necessarily the
creator's original uploads. The 19 prepared excerpts are trimmed, resampled
to mono 24 kHz PCM16, edge-faded and gain-adjusted. All trim and gain values
are recorded in SOURCES.json. No voice cloning or pitch conversion is used.

FSD50K dataset attribution (separate CC BY 4.0 dataset license):
Eduardo Fonseca, Xavier Favory, Jordi Pons, Frederic Font and Xavier Serra,
Music Technology Group, Universitat Pompeu Fabra.
"FSD50K: An Open Dataset of Human-Labeled Sound Events", arXiv:2010.00475.
https://zenodo.org/records/4060432
See LICENSE-DATASET.txt for dataset terms.

The original preparation plan was lost during interrupted development;
its historical hash is retained in SOURCES.json, not rewritten. All
source references and excerpt parameters needed to reproduce the WAVs
survive in that manifest. tools/prepare_roster_cues.py verifies and
reproduces these files without claiming to be the original tool revision.

These recordings remain candidates pending human listening, clinical
content, privacy/publicity, and character-fit review. CC0 does not verify
performer age, gender, illness, clinical accuracy or consent for voice
cloning. No creator endorsement is implied. The full-roster release gate
remains closed until the separate hash-bound review is completed.
